# Healing Guide

**Healing Guide is a comprehensive healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between patients, medical professionals, and healthcare facilities. The platform consists of three main components working seamlessly together to improve healthcare accessibility and management.**

### 🏥 For Healthcare Facilities

A user-friendly web portal where medical facilities can:

- Manage their facility profiles and services
- List available medical departments and specialties
- Update operational information and availability
- Monitor patient feedback and ratings
- Handle appointment requests
- Showcase their medical staff and expertise

### 📱 For Patients (Mobile App)

An intuitive mobile application enabling patients to:

- Find and compare nearby medical facilities
- Search for specialists based on medical conditions
- Access detailed information about medical procedures
- Book appointments with healthcare providers
- Share experiences through ratings and reviews
- Track their medical visits
- Access healthcare resources in their preferred language

### 👨‍⚕️ For Physicians (Mobile App)

A dedicated mobile interface for healthcare professionals to:

-  Manage their professional profiles
-  View and handle patient appointments
-  Access patient feedback
-  Update their availability and specialties
-  Connect with medical facilities
-  Share their expertise and credentials
